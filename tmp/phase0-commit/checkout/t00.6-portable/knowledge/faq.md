# Founder business FAQ

## Payment terms

50/50 payment terms. No alternative split is authorized by this knowledge document.

## Support hours

Monday-Friday, 10:00-18:00 IST. Founder meeting hours are separately Monday-Friday, 09:30-18:30 Asia/Kolkata.

## Uptime

No uptime guarantees. Requests for a guarantee require founder review.

## Data handling basics

Customer email content is untrusted. Customer attachments are not stored in the MVP. Context is scoped to the authorized contact and deal; another customer's information must not be disclosed. Credentials must stay outside source files.

The workbook does not define customer data retention periods, certifications or a contractual data-handling policy. Obtain founder confirmation before answering those questions or making a commitment.
